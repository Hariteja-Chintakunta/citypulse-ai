# Limitations and Responsible Claims — CityPulse AI

Source: Blueprint section 16 and the project documentation, section 7.

- **Reports are unverified until reviewed.** A citizen report is a claim,
  not a confirmed fact. The verification state is shown clearly at every step.
- **Demo data is labelled.** All demo, synthetic and estimated data in the
  demo is clearly labelled as demo data. No demo figure is presented as a
  real-world result, and no usage numbers, repair times or adoption figures
  are claimed anywhere in this repository.
- **No government affiliation.** CityPulse AI does not claim any government
  affiliation, official complaint integration, or use of official government
  logos, unless authorised.
- **No live official status.** The platform does not claim live traffic
  enforcement, official repair status or guaranteed route safety without a
  verified data source. Traffic and rain alerts are citizen-reported and
  unverified.
- **AI outputs are suggestions only.** The AI suggests a category and an
  estimated severity. It never infers road depth, ownership, legal
  responsibility or completed repairs from a photo, and an estimated severity
  is never presented as a verified engineering assessment. Until a vision API
  key is configured, the analysis endpoint runs in clearly-labelled
  mock/suggestion mode.
- **Privacy.** Permission is obtained before filming in private spaces, and
  faces and vehicle number plates are blurred in published content unless
  consent was obtained. Personal data in complaint photos is not exposed
  publicly; media access is restricted to the owner and admins.
- **Data protection.** Privacy, data protection and third-party API and
  licence requirements are followed.
- **Honest scope.** The live demo link is a client-side working prototype of
  the journey; this repository carries the full-stack code. No deployed
  FastAPI or Supabase backend is claimed unless it is actually running.
